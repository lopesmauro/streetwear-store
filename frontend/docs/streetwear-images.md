# Imagens — forma / Drop 01

Imagens geradas com a ferramenta integrada image_gen (habilidade imagegen), para a loja fictícia. Fotografias de campanha e produtos ilustrativos, sem associação com produtos reais.

Arquivos finais consumidos pelo site, em public/images/: streetwear-campaign.webp, hoodie.webp, tee.webp, cargo.webp e cap.webp. Os PNGs originais foram preservados no diretório de geração da ferramenta; as versões WebP são apenas otimizações de formato para carregamento.

## Prompts finais

### streetwear-campaign.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero photograph for a Brazilian streetwear store called forma. Create one premium natural editorial fashion photograph, landscape 4:3. Two adult models, one man and one woman, wearing contemporary understated streetwear: oversized heavyweight washed forest-green (#2c7036) hoodie, black baggy cargo trousers, off-white boxy t-shirt, charcoal loose denim, clean low-top skate sneakers. They stand casually on raw concrete steps outside a modern concrete building in a Brazilian city. Authentic relaxed attitudes, interesting low angle, natural skin and cotton textures, overcast daylight, restrained film grain, muted urban concrete and forest green palette. Full bodies and clothes clearly visible, subjects centered so image can be cropped slightly on sides. No text, no graphic overlay, no visible logos, no watermark. Realistic campaign photography, not a collage or webpage.

### hoodie.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: individual streetwear e-commerce product photograph, vertical 3:4. Subject: washed forest-green (#2c7036) oversized heavyweight cotton pullover hoodie, dropped shoulders, kangaroo pocket, no drawstrings. One item only, entire item fully visible and centered with generous margin on all sides, laid out carefully on a light neutral concrete-gray studio surface. Premium realistic catalog photography with natural cotton texture and soft directional daylight shadows. Flat lay top-down photography, no people, no hangers, no surrounding props, no graphic overlays, no text except requested cap embroidery, no watermark. Understated modern streetwear aesthetic, consistent light gray background #e7e7e2.
